Privacy Policy

How Nuventra collects, uses and protects personal information.

Privacy Policy

Nuventra Limited is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information securely, fairly and responsibly.

Who We Are

Nuventra Limited is the Data Controller for personal information we process in connection with our website and services.

Information We May Collect

  • Name, address and contact details
  • Account and financial information where relevant
  • Communication records
  • Information customers provide about personal circumstances
  • Website usage data, IP address and browser information

How We Use Information

  • To communicate with customers
  • To manage accounts and customer support
  • To consider financial difficulty or vulnerability
  • To respond to enquiries and complaints
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve our services

Sharing Information

We may share information with professional advisers, IT providers, tracing agents, legal representatives, regulators or public authorities where lawful and necessary.

We do not sell personal data.

Your Rights

You have rights under UK GDPR including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal information.

To exercise your rights, contact customer@nuventra.co.uk.

ICO

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal information, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
