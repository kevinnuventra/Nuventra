How Nuventra collects, uses and protects personal information.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Nuventra Limited ("Nuventra", "we", "us" or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in a fair, lawful, transparent and secure way.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we may share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under UK data protection law.

Where We Get Your Information

We may receive personal information from:

  • You directly, when you contact us by email, telephone, post, online form or any other method
  • A third party authorised to act on your behalf
  • Creditors, lenders, service providers or account owners where lawful and appropriate
  • Credit reference agencies
  • Tracing or address-verification providers
  • Publicly available sources, such as Companies House, public registers or insolvency records
  • Professional advisers, solicitors, courts or enforcement bodies where relevant

We only collect information that is necessary for the purposes for which it is being processed.

What Information We Collect

Depending on the circumstances, we may process the following categories of personal information:

Identity and Contact Information

  • Name
  • Address
  • Date of birth, where required for identification
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Account or reference numbers

Account and Financial Information

  • Account balances
  • Payment history
  • Repayment arrangements
  • Affordability information where provided
  • Banking details where voluntarily supplied for payment or verification purposes

Communication Records

  • Emails, letters and messages
  • Call notes and call recordings where applicable
  • Records of enquiries, complaints and customer support requests

Credit, Risk and Verification Information

  • Credit reference agency data
  • Address verification information
  • Fraud prevention and identity verification information
  • Information required to assess account status or customer circumstances

Vulnerability and Support Needs

You may choose to share information about your health, personal circumstances, financial difficulty, accessibility needs or other support requirements. We treat this information with particular care and only record what is necessary to support you appropriately.

Why We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To communicate with customers and authorised representatives
  • To manage accounts and customer enquiries
  • To understand customer circumstances and provide appropriate support
  • To consider financial difficulty, affordability or vulnerability
  • To verify identity and contact details
  • To prevent fraud, financial crime or misuse of our services
  • To handle complaints
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and reporting obligations
  • To maintain accurate records
  • To improve our systems, processes and customer experience

Lawful Basis for Processing

We only process personal information where we have a lawful basis under UK GDPR. Depending on the activity, this may include:

  • Legitimate interests – for example, managing accounts, communicating with customers, verifying information, preventing fraud and improving services
  • Legal obligation – where we are required to keep records, respond to lawful requests or comply with applicable laws
  • Contract – where processing is necessary in connection with a contract or agreement
  • Consent – where consent is required, such as certain types of sensitive information or specific communication preferences

How We Support Customers in Vulnerable Circumstances

We understand that life events can make financial matters more difficult. If you tell us that you need additional support, or if we believe additional support may be needed, we may use your information to:

  • Tailor communications to your needs
  • Allow extra time to respond
  • Pause activity while we review your circumstances
  • Consider affordable and sustainable options
  • Signpost you to free, independent debt advice organisations where appropriate
  • Allow an authorised third party to communicate with us on your behalf

Where information relates to health or other sensitive matters, we will handle it carefully, restrict access, and only keep what is necessary.

Who We Share Your Information With

We may share information where lawful and necessary with:

  • Authorised staff and contractors
  • Credit reference agencies
  • Tracing and address-verification providers
  • IT, hosting, telephony and secure communication providers
  • Mailing, printing or document management providers
  • Solicitors, courts, enforcement agents or professional advisers where appropriate
  • Regulators, public authorities or law enforcement where required by law
  • Any third party you authorise to act on your behalf

We do not sell personal data and we do not share personal data for unrelated marketing purposes.

International Transfers

Our default position is to keep personal information within the UK or EEA. If information must be transferred outside the UK or EEA, we will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as adequacy regulations, approved contractual clauses, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

How We Protect Your Information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Secure systems and networks
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Staff training and confidentiality obligations
  • Secure retention and deletion processes
  • Monitoring and review of data protection controls

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including account management, legal obligations, complaint handling, audit, reporting, dispute resolution and fraud prevention.

When information is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted, anonymised or destroyed.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have rights in relation to your personal information. These may include:

  • The right to be informed
  • The right of access
  • The right to rectification
  • The right to erasure, where applicable
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to object to certain processing
  • The right to data portability, where applicable
  • Rights relating to automated decision-making and profiling, where applicable

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
