Data Protection & UK GDPR Policy

Nuventra Limited is committed to complying with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable data protection requirements.

Our Role

Nuventra Limited acts as Data Controller for personal data processed in connection with our website, customer communications and business activities.

Lawful Bases For Processing

Depending on the activity, we may process personal information under legitimate interests, legal obligation, contract, or consent where appropriate.

Types Of Personal Data

  • Identification and contact details
  • Account and financial information where relevant
  • Communication records
  • Support and vulnerability-related information provided by customers
  • Website and technical data

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Data Breaches

Any suspected data security incident will be investigated, recorded and managed promptly. Where required, we will notify the ICO and affected individuals.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for lawful business purposes, legal obligations, dispute resolution and record keeping.

Customer Rights

Customers have rights under UK GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability where applicable.
