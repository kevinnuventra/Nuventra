Nuventra Rewards Programme

Supporting your financial progress, one positive step at a time.

Welcome To Nuventra Rewards

At Nuventra Limited, we believe customers should be supported, encouraged and treated with fairness when working towards resolving outstanding balances.

Our Rewards Programme is designed to recognise positive engagement and help customers feel more in control of their journey.

There is no pressure and no obligation. Rewards are designed to support progress, not to encourage unaffordable payments.

How Rewards Work

Customers may be able to earn rewards by taking positive steps such as:

  • Making consistent affordable payments
  • Keeping us updated about their circumstances
  • Providing updated income and expenditure information where appropriate
  • Engaging with us when we contact them
  • Letting us know if their situation changes

Reward Types

£

Monthly Progress Rewards

Consistent affordable payments may help customers qualify for rewards that support balance reduction.

⏱

Payment Holiday Support

Where appropriate, customers may be able to request temporary payment flexibility if circumstances change.

↻

Payment Plan Reviews

If a customer’s situation changes, we may review their arrangement to ensure it remains realistic and sustainable.

✓

Settlement Consideration

Customers who remain engaged may be able to discuss settlement options where appropriate and affordable.

Fairness First

Rewards will never be used to pressure customers into paying more than they can afford.

If a customer is experiencing financial difficulty or vulnerability, our priority will always be support, understanding and appropriate adjustment.
