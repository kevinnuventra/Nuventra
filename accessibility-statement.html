Accessibility Statement

Nuventra Limited is committed to ensuring our services are accessible to all customers.

We recognise that individuals have different communication needs and we are committed to making reasonable adjustments wherever possible.

Our Commitment

  • Provide information in a clear and understandable format
  • Communicate respectfully and fairly
  • Support customers with disabilities or health conditions
  • Offer alternative communication methods where appropriate
  • Remove barriers that may prevent customers from engaging with us

Alternative Communication Options

Where appropriate, we may be able to offer email communication, written correspondence, larger text formats, extended response times or communication through an authorised third party.

Website Accessibility

We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website and digital services. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, please contact us and we will do our best to assist.
