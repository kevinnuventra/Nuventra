Our Approach

Clear communication, fair treatment and positive customer outcomes.

Customer First

Our approach is built around listening, understanding and helping customers identify realistic ways forward.

What Customers Can Expect

  • Clear and respectful communication
  • Time to explain personal circumstances
  • Support where financial difficulty or vulnerability is identified
  • Signposting to free independent debt advice where appropriate
  • Fair consideration of affordable options

How We Work

We aim to avoid jargon and provide information in a way that is easy to understand. If a customer needs extra support, more time or an alternative communication method, we will do our best to make reasonable adjustments.
