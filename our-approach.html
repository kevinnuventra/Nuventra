Our Approach

Understanding circumstances, communicating clearly and treating customers fairly.

Our Approach

Nuventra Limited believes that every customer situation should be considered on its own merits. We understand that financial circumstances can be influenced by a wide range of personal, economic and unexpected factors.

Our role is to engage with customers professionally, communicate clearly and consider individual circumstances when discussing account resolution.

We aim to create a constructive environment where customers feel able to engage openly and access appropriate support where needed.

Understanding Individual Circumstances

No two customers are the same. Circumstances can change due to employment, health, family commitments, rising living costs or other life events.

Before considering potential solutions, we encourage customers to share any information they believe may be relevant to their situation. This helps us better understand their circumstances and identify suitable options.

Constructive Communication

We believe customers should always have access to clear and accurate information.

Our communications aim to explain:

  • The account being discussed
  • Who we are and why we are contacting you
  • Available contact methods
  • Potential options that may be available
  • Sources of independent support and advice

We aim to communicate in a professional, respectful and straightforward manner.

Practical Resolution Options

Where appropriate, we will discuss realistic and sustainable options that take account of a customer's circumstances.

Our objective is not simply to seek payment, but to understand whether support, flexibility or alternative arrangements may be appropriate.

Supporting Customers Who Need Additional Assistance

Some customers may require additional support due to health conditions, financial hardship, bereavement, caring responsibilities or other personal circumstances.

Where appropriate, we will consider reasonable adjustments, alternative communication methods and additional time to help customers engage with us effectively.

Further information is available within our Financial Difficulty and Vulnerability Support pages.

Our Core Principles

Fair Treatment

Every customer should be treated professionally, respectfully and consistently.

Transparency

Information should be clear, accurate and easy to understand.

Understanding

Individual circumstances should be considered wherever appropriate.

Accountability

We regularly review our processes, communications and customer outcomes to identify opportunities for improvement.
