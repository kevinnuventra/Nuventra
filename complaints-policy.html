Complaints Policy

At Nuventra Limited, we are committed to treating every customer fairly, respectfully and with care. If something has gone wrong, we want to know so we can investigate and put things right where appropriate.

Nuventra Limited is currently progressing an application for FCA authorisation. Whilst authorisation is pending, we are not currently FCA authorised and we do not state that complaints can be referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service unless a specific legal right applies.

We have adopted complaint handling, customer care and vulnerability standards aligned with recognised industry best practice.

What Is A Complaint?

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our service, staff, actions or inaction where a response or resolution is expected.

How We Handle Complaints

  • We will listen and take your concerns seriously
  • We will acknowledge your complaint as soon as reasonably possible
  • We will investigate fairly and objectively
  • We will keep you updated where further time is required
  • We will provide a clear response explaining our findings

We aim to resolve complaints as quickly as possible and, wherever practical, within five working days.

Vulnerable Customers

If you need additional support when making a complaint, we will make reasonable adjustments and communicate in a way that works for you wherever possible.

Learning From Complaints

We record complaints, review trends and use feedback to improve our processes, communication and customer experience.

Helping customers find fair, sustainable and positive financial outcomes through clear communication and respectful support.
