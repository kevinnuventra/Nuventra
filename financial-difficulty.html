Financial Difficulty

At Nuventra Limited, we understand that financial circumstances can change unexpectedly.

Life events such as illness, redundancy, reduced income, relationship breakdown, bereavement or rising living costs can make it difficult to manage financial commitments.

If you are experiencing financial difficulty, we encourage you to contact us as soon as possible. The earlier we understand your circumstances, the more options may be available.

How We Can Help

  • Review your circumstances
  • Discuss affordable and sustainable options
  • Consider additional time where appropriate
  • Signpost free independent debt advice
  • Consider extra support where vulnerability is identified

What We May Ask For

To better understand your circumstances, we may ask for information about income, expenditure or changes to your situation. Any information you provide will be handled confidentially and in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Independent Debt Advice

If you require free debt advice, we encourage you to contact one of the organisations listed on our Debt Advice Organisations page.
