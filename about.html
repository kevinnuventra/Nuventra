About Nuventra

A UK company preparing for FCA authorisation and focused on fair customer support.

Who We Are

Nuventra Limited is a UK-based company focused on fair customer engagement, clear communication and practical account resolution.

We recognise that financial circumstances can change and that each customer situation should be considered individually. Our aim is to communicate professionally, understand relevant circumstances and discuss suitable options where appropriate.

Our Status

Nuventra Limited is currently progressing an application for FCA authorisation. We are not currently FCA authorised and do not describe ourselves as authorised or regulated by the FCA.

Whilst authorisation is pending, we have adopted customer care, vulnerability and complaint handling standards aligned with recognised industry best practice.
